the english-speaking padel podcast

started by conner and hug after becoming obsessed with a sport that seemed impossible to stop talking about.

as english-speaking fans, we felt there was a gap for content that captured the conversations happening around padel: the players, rivalries, storylines and personalities that make people fall in love with the sport.

in 18 months it has grown into a platform reaching over 100,000 monthly impressions across podcast and social, connecting us with fans, players and guests from around the world.

we exist to cover padel in a way that feels authentic to the people who play it, follow it and care about where it's heading.
