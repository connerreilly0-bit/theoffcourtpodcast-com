Santiago P1 Recap: No Finals, Fast Courts & Fresh Drama

spotifyapple podcastsamazon musicyoutubeother apps

The Santiago P1 tournament will go down as one of the strangest in Premier Padel history. No men’s final, a women’s final cut short, lightning-fast courts, and a controversial walkover title for Chingotto & Galán — there’s a lot to unpack. 😮‍💨

In this episode, we break down:

- The chaotic end to Santiago P1

- Stupa & Lebrón’s sudden withdrawal due to illness

- The court speed that left lobs useless and turned the game into a battle of net dominance

- Cardona & Augsburger’s upset over Coello & Tapia

- Whether the Galan vs. Lebrón beef is heating up again

- The potential for pairing shakeups as the quarter season point hits

From chiquitas to chancletazos, this was a wild one.
