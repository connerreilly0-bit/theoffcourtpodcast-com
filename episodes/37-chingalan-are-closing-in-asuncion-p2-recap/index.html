Chingalan Are Closing In… Asunción P2 Recap

spotifyapple podcastsamazon musicyoutubeother apps

Brussels P2 gave us another huge weekend in the 2026 Premier Padel season, and the pressure at the top is starting to build.

Chingalan continue their charge in the race with another big tournament, while Coello & Tapia respond after Brussels and look back to their clinical best. But are the gaps between the top pairs starting to close?

We also break down the rise of the new partnerships making noise on tour, including Goñi & Alonso taking out Stupa/Yanguas, and what we think was the match of the tournament: Maxi Arce & Tello vs Chingalan. One of the most entertaining matches of the week and another sign that some of these new pairings could become serious threats moving forward.

On the women’s side, Bea González & Paula Josemaría make it FOUR wins in a row against Triay & Brea. At what point do they become the favourites heading into the rest of the season?
