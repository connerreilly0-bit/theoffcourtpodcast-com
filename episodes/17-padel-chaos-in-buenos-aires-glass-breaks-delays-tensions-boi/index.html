Padel Chaos in Buenos Aires: Glass Breaks, Delays, & Tensions Boil Over

spotifyapple podcastsamazon musicyoutubeother apps

This week, we break down all the drama, chaos, and standout moments from the Buenos Aires P1 — a tournament that somehow broke attendance records and broke down… literally.

💥 From shattered glass on the main court to matches being delayed and played behind closed doors, we dig into why this was one of the most poorly organised Premier Padel events of the season.

We also cover:

🔹 Chingotto & Galán dropping out after a brutal schedule

🔹 Stupa & Lebrón’s tense quarter-final loss — was Lebrón too fired up?

🔹 Upsets from Momo Sanz and Jofre Arroyo

🔹 Coello & Tapia’s dominant run (again!)

🔹 How 16,000 fans STILL turned up despite all the chaos

Was this tournament a total mess… or just part of the wild world of pro padel? We get into it.👇 Let us know your take in the comments – was Lebrón out of line, or just passionate?📍
