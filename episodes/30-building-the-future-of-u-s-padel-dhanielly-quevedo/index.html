Building the Future of U.S. Padel | Dhanielly Quevedo

spotifyapple podcastsamazon musicyoutubeother apps

In this episode of The Off-Court Podcast, we’re joined by Dhanielly “Dhany” Quevedo — Top 10 U.S. padel athlete and founder of Elite Sports Management — to break down what the future of padel in the United States really looks like.

We dive into Dhany’s journey from elite junior tennis to competing at the top of U.S. padel, why Miami has emerged as a major hub for the sport, and how padel is trying to carve out its place in a country dominated by pickleball. Dhany shares her perspective on what’s driving padel’s growth, what’s holding it back, and what needs to change for the sport to truly go mainstream in the U.S.

Beyond the court, we explore the business side of padel — from club models and athlete support to why Dhany decided to launch Elite Sports Management, the first female-owned, padel-focused sports agency in the country. We also talk about representation, leadership, and what it takes to balance life as both a high-level athlete and a founder.
