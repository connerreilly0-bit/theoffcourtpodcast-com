Spain's Coach Took a HUGE Gamble… and Won the World Cup | World Cup Review

spotifyapple podcastsamazon musicyoutubeother apps

Spain's coach took a huge gamble in the World Cup final, and it won Spain the title without Alejandro Galán.

In this World Cup review we break down the gamble that decided the men's final: Spain held Arturo Coello back for match 3 and sent Juan Lebrón and debutant Aimar Goñi out first against Leo Augsburger and Martín Di Nenno. Down a set and a break, they won it 5-7 7-5 6-2. Tapia and Chingotto levelled it 6-1 6-2, then Coello and Coki Nieto beat Tino Libaak and Gonzalo Alfonso 6-3 6-2 for Spain's fifth men's title.

On the women's side, Spain won their seventh World Cup in a row and dropped one set all week. Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero beat Osoro and Fassio 6-3 6-3, and Gemma Triay and Paula Josemaría closed it out 6-2 6-7 6-3 against Delfi Brea and Claudia Jensen. Plus Portugal and Italy's bronze medals, the Netherlands, Sweden and Germany in the quarter-finals for the first time, and Paquito Navarro's last World Cup?

Did Spain need Galán? Tell us in the comments 👇
