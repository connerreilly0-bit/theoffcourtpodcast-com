Premier Padel Barcelona Finals 2025 Breakdown

spotifyapple podcastsamazon musicyoutubeother apps

The Premier Padel season comes to an end in Barcelona, finishing with one of the best finals we’ve seen all year.

In this episode, we break down the 2025 Barcelona Finals, from Coello & Tapia’s dominant run to another unforgettable El Clásico against Galán & Chingotto — a match decided by millimetres, pressure moments, unforced errors, and one crucial break point.

We also cover key matchups across the men’s draw, including Stupa & Di Nenno’s statement win, Leal and Guerrero’s first Barcelona finals appearances, and what went wrong for Lebrón & Augsburger. On the women’s side, we look at Claudia Fernández & Bea González’s incredible run, the emotional final chapter of Sánchez & Josemaría, and what the next wave of pairings could look like heading into 2026.
