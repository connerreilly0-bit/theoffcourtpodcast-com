Chingalán WIN Madrid as Coello & Tapia CRASH OUT! | Madrid P1 Review

spotifyapple podcastsamazon musicyoutubeother apps

Madrid P1 delivered one of the wildest tournaments of the 2026 season.

Coello & Tapia were knocked out in the quarter-finals by Coki Nieto & Mike Yanguas, opening the door for Chingotto & Galán to claim the Madrid title and close the gap in the race for World No.1.

We break down everything that happened in Madrid, from the shock exits across the men’s draw to Chingalán’s title run and whether they can actually dethrone Coello & Tapia before the end of the season.

On the women’s side, Gemma Triay & Delfi Brea continued their dominance, while Paula Josemaría & Bea González produced an incredible comeback from 5-0 down in the deciding set against Claudia Fernández & Martina Calvo.

Plus, we look at the biggest winners and losers from Madrid and say farewell to a legend of the sport.
