Tapia Is Untouchable & Did Lebrón Celebrate Too Early? | Málaga P1 Review

spotifyapple podcastsamazon musicyoutubeother apps

The Málaga P1 gave us plenty to talk about.

This week on The Off-Court Podcast, we break down another dominant title run from Arturo Coello & Agustín Tapia, who claimed their fifth consecutive Premier Padel title. Is this the best version of Tapia we've ever seen?

We also dive into Juan Lebrón & Leo Augsburger's incredible run to the final after their emotional semi-final victory over Chingotto & Galán. But did that celebration come too soon, and did it have any impact on the final?

On the women's side, Gemma Triay & Delfi Brea edged Bea González & Paula Josemaría in an epic three-hour final. We also discuss Stupa & Yanguas' future as a pair, the latest partnership changes ahead of Pretoria P1, and Drake's investment into padel.

In this episode:

• Are Coello & Tapia at their peak?
 • Did Lebrón celebrate too early?
 • Is it time for Stupa & Yanguas to split?
 • Women's Final review.
 • New partnerships ahead of Pretoria P1.
 • Thoughts on the fast conditions in South Africa.
 • Drake enters the world of padel.
